One monologue of about 100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired: thirty years of the same speech, heroes arriving before the kettle, a crown that pinches, a scheme waiting on one misplaced quill — and a request to fight on Tuesday instead.

> Heroes. Stand there while I... where was I? Ah. The prophecy. Thirty years I've kept this speech warm, and still you arrive, before the kettle, before the ravens have filed their complaints. You want the crown? Take it. It pinches. It whispers at night. My scheme — the eclipse, the army of the hollow deep, all of it — waits on one signature, and the clerk, bless his rotten heart, misplaced the quill. Again. Kneel, if you must. Monologue, then. But slowly. My knees... Look, fight me Tuesday. The doom will keep. I just need five minutes. Eyes closed. Standing.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 600 characters of UTF-8 text, about 100 words.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
